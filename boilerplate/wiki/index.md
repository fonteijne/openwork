# Wiki index

Compiled knowledge goes here. Add entries as pages are created and keep this index useful as a navigation map; do not list raw sources as if they were knowledge pages.

## Topics

- [Knowledge compilation](knowledge-compilation.md) — source-grounded synthesis, provenance, review, and output flow.
- [Project glossary](glossary.md) — preferred terminology, definitions, aliases, and terms to avoid.
- [People](people/index.md) — project-relevant profiles; individual pages use `wiki/people/first-last.md`.

## Compilation principles

- Every material claim links to supporting source material in `../raw/`.
- Existing pages are searched before new ones are created.
- Conflicting or insufficient evidence is recorded in [`_review-queue.md`](_review-queue.md), not silently reconciled.
