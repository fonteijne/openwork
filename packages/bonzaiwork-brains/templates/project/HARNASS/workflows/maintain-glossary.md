# Maintain the project glossary

## Purpose

Keep project jargon and preferred terminology clear and consistent. A glossary may also act as a lightweight controlled vocabulary when it records preferred terms, aliases, and terms to avoid. Do not imply formal standards where the project has only an informal convention.

## 1. Inspect current terminology

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `wiki/index.md`, and `wiki/glossary.md`. Search relevant wiki pages and raw sources for the term and variants. Identify whether the requested meaning is:

- explicitly defined by a cited authoritative source;
- a project convention confirmed by an authorized person/team;
- one source's usage among competing usages; or
- currently uncertain.

Do not treat frequency of use as approval or authority.

## 2. Clarify and classify

Ask the user for missing information that changes the entry: preferred term, intended meaning in this project, aliases, contexts where usage differs, terms to avoid, and who/what establishes the definition. A definition can be source-backed, project-approved, provisional, or under review. Keep those states distinct.

When sources conflict or a team decision is needed, do not choose a preferred term silently. Add the question and evidence to `wiki/_review-queue.md` or present a proposed convention for explicit confirmation.

## 3. Draft the entry

Use the existing entry format in `wiki/glossary.md`. Include:

- preferred term;
- concise context-specific meaning;
- usage guidance;
- aliases, acronyms, and meaningful variants;
- terms to avoid and rationale, if any;
- evidence link and locator, or the confirming person/role and date;
- status (source-defined, confirmed, provisional, or under review).

Do not add personal opinions as definitions or claim a team-wide convention from one person's unapproved preference. If the requester has authority to set the convention, record that basis; otherwise ask who should confirm it.

## 4. Get approval where needed

Source-attributed definitions may be added with their source and scope clearly stated, while preserving disagreements. Before marking a preferred term, deprecation, or team convention as confirmed, show the exact entry to a person with appropriate authority and obtain approval. Until then label it provisional or under review.

## 5. Integrate and verify

Update `wiki/index.md` only if needed (the glossary should remain indexed). Link relevant knowledge pages when useful; do not edit every occurrence mechanically. Preserve source wording in quotations and do not rewrite historical evidence to match current preferred terminology. Re-read edits, verify cited sources/locators and links, and report what is confirmed versus unresolved.
