# Review project knowledge

## Purpose

Surface unresolved decisions, contradictions, weak support, and likely staleness so a person can decide what to do. This review does not itself certify all wiki content as correct.

## 1. Define review scope

Review the whole project or the area/date range requested. Read `wiki/index.md` and `wiki/_review-queue.md`. Inspect relevant wiki pages and linked raw sources; do not treat absence from the queue as proof that knowledge is sound.

## 2. Check for review signals

Look for:

- unresolved or ambiguously worded queue items;
- claims with missing, broken, or weak provenance;
- conflicting claims, scope mismatches, or unmarked uncertainty;
- time-sensitive information that may be outdated relative to its source date;
- pages marked provisional/draft or lacking clear project scope;
- duplicate pages or index/link drift that impairs navigation;
- glossary entries with unsupported definitions, missing provenance/approval status, unresolved competing terms, or inconsistent use in current outputs;
- person-page role or decision-scope details whose confirmation date may be stale.

Verify findings against sources where possible. Distinguish confirmed defects from possible risks and explain the evidence. Do not infer staleness from age alone when facts are stable.

## 3. Propose, don't silently decide

Present findings with location, evidence, impact, and a suggested next action. For disputed claims or decisions, keep alternatives visible and route them to the review queue. Do not mark unresolved items resolved, rewrite consequential claims as settled, or delete content without the relevant evidence and human decision.

## 4. Apply only clear, authorized maintenance

You may fix unambiguous mechanical issues (for example, a broken link when the correct target is certain) and report them. For substantive corrections, ask for approval or record a review item. If a human provides a decision, capture it with its authority, rationale, date, and affected knowledge before resolving the queue item.

## 5. Report

Summarize scope reviewed, findings by priority, any mechanical changes made, proposed actions, unresolved decisions, and limits of the review. Link directly to files and sources.
